Practical tools for independent game developers.

IndieWest is an independent game developer making focused Unity tools. Each one takes care of groundwork that many games share, and comes with documentation and a working sample, so you can spend your time on what makes your game different.

Card Hand Toolkit

Card Hand Toolkit gives your Unity card game polished hand layouts, responsive hover and drag interactions, and flexible card visuals. Start with the included sample and Setup Wizard, then adapt the toolkit to your own game.

  • Card hands, hover, dragging and playing cards into zones
  • Customizable card layouts, frames, art, text and game data
  • Setup Wizard, documentation and a complete sample scene
  • Unity 2023.1+ and Unity 6 · Built-in and URP · desktop, iOS and Android
